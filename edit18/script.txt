Aapke ad mein price nahi hai? Isi liye fake leads aa rahi hain.
Sach bataun, DM for price wale ads sabse zyada time waste karwate hain. Lead quality theek karne ke 4 tareeke.
Pehla. Ad mein price range seedha likho. Starting 85 lakh. Jo afford nahi kar sakta, woh click hi nahi karega. Aur yeh achhi baat hai.
Doosra. Lead form mein do sawaal jodo. Budget kya hai, aur ghar kab tak chahiye. Leads thodi kam aayengi, par sahi aayengi.
Teesra. DM for details hatao. Seedha likho, site visit book karo. Jo visit ke liye ready hai, wahi aage aaye.
Chautha. Retargeting sirf unko karo, jinhone aapka walkthrough video aadha dekha. Baaki pe paisa mat jalao.
Yaad rakho. Hazaar random leads se, sau sahi leads zyada deals laati hain.
