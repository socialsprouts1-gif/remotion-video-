Dosto, kisi ko paise dene se better hai ki jaakar kahin jua khel aaiye, ya kisi five-star hotel mein apne upar aur apne bachchon ke upar kharch kar dijiye.
Kyunki dosto, yahi toh aap log karte hain. Aap log kisi ko bhi jaane-anjaane mein paise de dete hain, bina yeh soche ki kya hum waqai kisi ko apna portfolio manage karne ke liye paise de rahe hain?
Dosto, market mein yeh sab scams chal rahe hain. Kai jagah toh aisa bhi dekha gaya hai ki meri photo laga kar Telegram par bahut saare scams chal rahe hain.
Toh dosto, yeh paisa aapka hai, aapke bachchon ka hai. Kyunki jab aap loss lete hain na, dosto, aur in scams mein phans jaate hain, toh apni khoon-paseene ki mehnat aur gaadhe ki kamai kisi ko de dete hain, yeh sochkar ki woh hamare paison ko multiply karke wapas karega.
Arey dosto, agar use paise multiply karna hi aata, toh woh aapse paise kyun leta? Woh khud crorepati ya arabpati nahi ban jaata?
Yeh baat samajhna mushkil nahi hai. Kyunki yeh paisa aapka hai, aapke bachchon ka hai. Toh kripya karke kisi ko bhi apne paise mat dijiye.
Aur jahan meri photo lagi ho, wahan toh bilkul bhi paise mat dijiye, kyunki dosto, hum kisi se koi paisa nahi lete hain.
Paise dete waqt yeh zaroor dekh lijiye ki aap kis naam par paise de rahe hain. Kya waqai aap Deepak Wadhwa ko paise de rahe hain, ya kisi Shyamlal, Biharilal ko?
Agar aisa hai, toh scam hona nishchit hai.
Apne paison ko bachaiye, kyunki in paison par haq sirf aapke bachchon ka hai.
