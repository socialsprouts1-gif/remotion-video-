Dosto, kisi ko paise dene se behtar hai ki jaakar kahin jua khel aaiye, ya kisi five-star hotel mein apne upar aur apne bachchon ke upar kharch kar dijiye. Kyunki dosto, yahi toh aap log karte hain. Aap log kisi ko bhi jaane-anjaane mein paise de dete hain, yeh soche bagair ki kya yeh paisa waqai hum kisi ko de rahe hain, portfolio manage karne ke liye? Dosto, yeh sab scam chal raha hai bazaar mein. Kai jagah toh aisa dekha gaya hai ki meri photo laga ke bhi Telegram pe bahut saare scam chal rahe hain. Toh yeh paisa aapka hai, aapke bachchon ka hai. Kyunki jab aap loss lete hain na, dosto, in scams mein phans jaate hain, apni khoon-paseene ki mehnat, gaadhe ki kamai kisi ko de dete hain, yeh soch ke ki woh hamare paise ko multiply karke wapas karega. Dosto, agar use multiply karna hi aata, toh woh aapse paisa kyun leta? Woh khud crorepati, arabpati nahi ban jaata? Yeh baat samajhna mushkil nahi hai. Kyunki yeh paisa aapka hai, aapke bachchon ka hai. Kripya karke kisi ko paisa na dein. Jahan meri photo lagi ho, wahan toh bilkul na dein, kyunki dosto, hum kisi se koi paisa nahi lete hain. Paisa dete waqt yeh dekh lijiye ki kis naam se de rahe hain. Kya waqai Deepak Wadhwa ko paisa de rahe hain, ya kisi Shyamlal, Biharilal ko? Yadi aisa hai, toh scam hona nishchit hai. Apne paise ko bachaiye, kyunki in paison pe haq sirf aapke bachchon ka hai.
