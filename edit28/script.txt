Customer dimaag se nahi, dil se kharidta hai. Aur phir dimaag se justify karta hai.
Yeh 5 psychology triggers samajh lo, aapki sales badal jayegi.
Ek. Social proof. Jahan 500 reviews hote hain, wahan naya customer bina soche bharosa karta hai.
Do. Scarcity. Sirf 3 slots bache hain, yeh padhte hi decision fast ho jaata hai.
Teen. Anchoring. Pehle 9,999 dikhao, phir 4,999. Doosra price apne aap sasta lagta hai.
Chaar. Teen options do. Log aksar beech wala choose karte hain, isliye apna best plan beech mein rakho.
Paanch. Pehle free value do. Ek tip, ek guide, ek sample. Insaan badle mein kuch dena chahta hai.
Yaad rakho, yeh manipulation nahi hai. Jo business customer ko samajhta hai, wahi zyada bechta hai.
