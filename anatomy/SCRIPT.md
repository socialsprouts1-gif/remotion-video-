# The Anatomy of a Reply — script

**Idea:** a premium product-commercial teardown. One customer message is the hero object. We slow a single moment down and travel inside NEURAXINE as it handles it, chapter by chapter, in real 3D: understand → recall → reply → qualify → book. Then pull back: now do that for every message.

40s · 1080×1920 · English · tone: Apple-keynote calm, precise, confident.
No invented numbers or claims; the business content (packages, times) is sample data inside the demo.
