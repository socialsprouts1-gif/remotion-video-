Is ek galti ki wajah se businesses apne lakhs of rupees ka nuksaan kar rahe hain.
Businesses Meta Ads par bahut saare paise spend kar rahe hain. Leads aa rahi hain, calls aa rahi hain, WhatsApp messages bhi aa rahe hain… lekin sales nahi badh rahi.
Problem aapke ads mein nahi hai. Problem hai aapke conversion system mein.
Aaj leads generate karna difficult nahi hai. Asli game hai un leads ko paying customers mein convert karna.
Customer WhatsApp par message karta hai, reply late milta hai, follow-up time par nahi hota… aur customer aapke competitor ke paas chala jaata hai.
Yahi ek galti businesses ko har mahine lakhs of rupees ka loss karwa rahi hai.
Aaj ke time mein most businesses ki conversations WhatsApp par hoti hain. Agar aapke paas ek proper follow-up system nahi hai, toh aap apna revenue lose kar rahe ho.

Automatic replies, smart follow-ups, lead qualification aur appointment booking — sab kuch ek hi jagah, taaki aapki inquiries customers mein convert ho sakein.
