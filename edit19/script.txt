Problem kapdon mein nahi hai. Problem marketing mein hai. Clothing stores ke liye 5 strategies.

Teen. Reels mein sirf kapde nahi, try-on dikhao. Real customers, real fitting, mirror selfie wala vibe.
Chaar. Google par saree shop near me search hota hai. Apni Google profile par photos aur reviews daalo.
Paanch. Birthday aur anniversary par ek chhota offer bhejo. Customer ko lagta hai, aapne yaad rakha.
Aur sach bataun? Naya customer laane se sasta hai, purane customer ko wapas bulana.
