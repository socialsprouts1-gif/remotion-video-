Leads aa rahi hain, par sale nahi ho rahi? Problem marketing mein nahi, follow-up mein hai.
Yeh 5 galtiyan check karo.
Ek. Late reply. Jo business 5 minute mein reply karta hai, uske close hone ke chances kai guna zyada hote hain.
Do. Sirf ek follow-up. Zyadatar sales teesre ya chauthe follow-up ke baad hoti hai, par log pehle pe hi ruk jaate hain.
Teen. Price pehle, value baad mein. Pehle problem aur result dikhao, phir price batao.
Chaar. Koi proof nahi. Reviews, before after, client stories. Bina proof ke bharosa nahi banta.
Paanch. Next step clear nahi. Har message ke end mein batao, ab kya karna hai. Call book karo, ya visit karo.
Leads laana mehenga hai, sambhalna sasta. Jo follow-up jeet leta hai, wahi market jeet leta hai.
