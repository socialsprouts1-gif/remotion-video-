# The Night Shift — script

**Idea:** your customers don't keep office hours. At 2:14 AM a customer wants to book; you're asleep, but your business isn't.
NEURAXINE answers, books, and then works through the whole day on a 24-hour dial: follow-ups, qualification, answers, campaigns, reminders.
Every action shown is a feature in the dashboard (AI assistant / chatbots, Appointments, Drip Campaigns, Leads, FAQ Bot, Bulk Campaigns, Reminders). The film shows no invented numbers.

Length 37s · 1080×1920 · English · tone: calm, confident, a little cinematic.
