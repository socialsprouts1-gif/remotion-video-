Real estate mein leads ki kami nahi hai. Site visits ki kami hai.
Dekho, 500 leads aur sirf 5 site visits, yeh marketing nahi, wastage hai. Yeh 5 strategies try karo.
Ek. Har project ke liye ek hi ad mat chalao. 2BHK buyer aur investor ka sawaal alag hota hai. Dono ke liye alag ad, alag message.
Do. Reel mein flat nahi, life dikhao. Subah ki dhoop, balcony se view, school kitna door hai. Log ghar nahi, apna future khareedte hain.
Teen. Google Business Profile ko seriously lo. Flats near me search karne wala buyer already ready hai. Photos, reviews, location, sab updated rakho.
Chaar. Har enquiry ka reply 5 minute ke andar. Late reply matlab, lead kisi aur builder ke paas.
Paanch. Site visit ke baad follow-up mat chhodo. Floor plan, EMI calculation, construction update, har hafte kuch kaam ka bhejo.
Aur sach bataun? Number paanch sabse underrated hai. Bahut saari deals pehli visit pe nahi, follow-up pe close hoti hain.
